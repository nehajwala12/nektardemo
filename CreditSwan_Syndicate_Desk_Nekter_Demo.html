What happens between upload and decision

Every document, every field

Spec sheets, interviews, P&Ls, AP agings, credit reports, bank statements and the fund's own ledger are parsed into a single fact table with a source reference on every number.

RECONCILE

The package against itself

Each management representation is matched to the document that should support it. Where the narrative and the source disagree, the row lands in the ledger.

TEST

Your criteria, your thresholds

Coverage, cushion, position, payables, concentration and documentation are scored against the policy on the Criteria tab, with the fund's own default history as the benchmark.

DECIDE

A verdict, the questions to put to the originator, the evidence that would clear them, and a drafted memo. The decision stays with you.
